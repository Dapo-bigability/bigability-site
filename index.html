The problem

Where work usually gets stuck

Most slowdowns come from a few familiar habits: paperwork, hand-offs and numbers no one can find. Here's what we replace them with.

  • Paper and PDF formsOnline intake that collects complete information
  • Hand-offs by email and phoneWorkflows that route work to the right person
  • Spreadsheets passed aroundOne shared, up-to-date record
  • Chasing status updatesDashboards that show what's waiting and what's late
  • Monthly scramble for numbersReports that arrive on schedule
Services

Consulting that ends in something that works

We start by understanding the process, then recommend and build the simplest fix. Most engagements combine a few of these.

Process review

We map how work actually gets done today, talk with the people doing it, and show you where time and information get lost.

  • Current-state maps
  • Quick wins
  • Prioritized plan

  • Uploads and PDFs

How we work

Understand the work first, then build

  1. Listen

    We meet with you and the people who do the work to understand what happens today and what's getting in the way.

  2. Map and recommend

    You get a simple map of the current process and a short list of changes, starting with the ones that help most.

  3. Build and test

    We build the forms, workflows and reports, test them with your staff, and adjust before anything goes live.

  4. Launch and hand off

    We roll it out, train your team and leave a plain-English guide so you're not dependent on us.

Built around your processWe fit the tools to how your team works, not the other way around.
On tools you already useWherever we can, we build on systems you already have, like Google Workspace or Microsoft 365.
